BRAINROT Game Jam 2025

The coming generation of developers will have an unprecedented amount of computing power and will revolutionize the way we live be absolutely cooked. At VikingsDev, we've decided to embrace the BRAINROT.

Sign Up! Join our Discord!

How Does It Work?

We're looking for the most creative, fun, and BRAINROTTED games. Whether it's a tralalero tralala platformer or a skibidi toilet simulator, we want the cringe, and the short attention span, turned up to the maximum! You can make any kind of game: GameMaker, Unity, Scratch, heck, even a board game or a choose-your-own-adventure book! Just make sure it's school appropriate, because teachers will be judging your entries.

Frequently Asked Questions

ts pmo fr

What is a game jam?

A game jam is an event where participants submit games. This year, our theme is BRAINROT.

When is the game jam happening?

The Brainrot Game Jam will be from June 2nd to June 18th!

Where is the game jam?

We will have two in-person optional meetings after school per week during the game jam in Mr. Janetka's room, room 2118. We will also have an opening and closing ceremony, where we explain the game jam and give out awards! For more information, join our Discord server: Click Here!

How do I submit?

The game jam will be hosted on itch.io.

Who can participate?

All current students of Burnaby North Secondary School (BNSS) can join the game jam!

What do I get for participating?

We will have stickers and candy during our in-person meetings, and prizes for a variety of different categories!

Who is judging?

Winners will be decided by the VikingsDev executive team and also a few guest teachers who will play (and attempt to understand) your games!

Do I need to know how to code to participate?

Nope! We'll teach you how to code in our afterschool sessions, or you can make a Scratch game, or even an offline game of some kind.
